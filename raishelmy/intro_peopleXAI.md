# Self Introduction — PeopleX.ai Interview
Structure: Present – Past – Future, built using 4 Skills / 3 Experience / 2 Achievements / 1 Reason to Hire Me.
Tone: simple, spoken, confident — not a presentation.

Interview flow hint: 1. Introduction (knowledge/skillset, routine task) → 2. Experience → 3. Your Questions.

Thank you so much for having me here today, I'm really excited for this interview.

My name is Rais Helmy. I'm originally from Semporna, a small town in Sabah, and I studied at Universiti Malaysia Sabah. I got married in 2021, I have one son, and right now I'm based in Kota Damansara.

## PRESENT — who I am now (4 skills)

Professionally, I'm a full stack engineer with over six years of experience, and a good part of that has been building enterprise workflow systems — which lines up closely with what PeopleX does in HRMS and payroll. Four things I'm strongest at:

1. Backend and API development with .NET — building the business logic behind approval and transactional workflows.
2. Approval workflow and routing management — designing multi-level approval chains and routing logic that move a request from submission to sign-off.
3. Dashboard and reporting — building dashboards that give visibility into claims, approvals, and process status at a glance.
4. Enterprise system integration — connecting these workflow systems to SAP, LDAP, and other core systems, the same way HRMS needs to integrate with payroll and identity systems.

Day to day, my routine has been fairly standard full stack work — picking up tickets, building and testing API endpoints, handling approval/routing logic changes, fixing bugs from UAT, and syncing with the team through stand-ups and sprint planning.

## PAST — where that came from (3 experiences)

I built these skills across three main experiences.

First, and most relevant here, at TNB through atQuest, I built HRMS-style modules end to end — claims, verification letter generation, approval workflows, routing management, and dashboards. This is essentially process automation for a workforce, which is the same core problem HRMS solves.

Second, still with TNB but through JuruQuest, my focus shifted to asset management systems — tracking and managing physical assets rather than employee-facing processes, so it gave me the other side of enterprise systems: inventory and asset lifecycle instead of workflow and approvals.

Third, at Etiqa Insurance, I worked on compliance systems that had to meet strict regulatory requirements from Bank Negara — which taught me how to build systems where accuracy, audit trail, and approval discipline really matter, the same discipline payroll and claims systems demand.

## PAST — proof it worked (2 achievements)

Two moments stand out.

On the atQuest side, I delivered the claim and approval workflow system that TNB staff used to submit and route claims and verification letters — cutting down manual, paper-based routing into a system with clear approval stages and dashboard visibility for tracking.

At Etiqa, I joined a project that was already a year behind schedule, and I got it delivered within two months — showing I can take over a struggling workflow-heavy system and push it to completion.

## FUTURE — why you should hire me (1 reason)

If I had to give you one reason to hire me: I've already built the exact building blocks of HRMS — claims, approvals, routing, verification letters, dashboards — in a real enterprise environment at TNB. I don't need to learn what these modules are for, I've built them. And I always push to exceed expectations and get things delivered properly, not just finished — which is the mindset I want to bring to PeopleX and its customers.
